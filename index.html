      05 Built from Within

      Built from within sport.

      Sport Within is built by two co-founders completing their MSc in Sport Business Management at Leeds Beckett University. Millions follow sport. Very few see how it works. We connect both sides.

      Co-founder

      Sahil Deshpande

      Sahil has spent his career where sport meets business. He ran operations across seven fitness facilities, led a team of around 60 people, then moved into business development and launched his own ed-tech startup. Now completing his MSc in Sport Business Management at Leeds Beckett University, he is building Sport Within to open the industry’s doors for the next generation of talent.

      Co-founder

      Devvrat Bhalerao

      Devvrat has built his career where sport, brand and growth meet. He led marketing for the biggest cricket museum in India and brings a strong business development background to everything he does. Now completing his MSc in Sport Business Management at Leeds Beckett University, he is helping build Sport Within into the community the next generation of sport professionals needs.
